Colophon

THE INSTRUMENT was written, designed and typeset in 2026. The text is set in EB Garamond, Georg Duffner’s revival of Claude Garamond’s sixteenth-century romans, with Instrument Serif for display and Instrument Sans for the working pages, both by Rodrigo Fuenzalida and Jordan Egstad. All three are released under the SIL Open Font License.

Printed in two colours, black and cinnabar, on Munken Pure Rough 120 gsm, an uncoated paper chosen because it takes a fountain pen. Sewn in sixteen-page signatures with a hollow back so that the book opens flat for writing. Bound in cloth over boards, blocked in copper foil, with printed endpapers, two ribbon markers and a cloth clamshell box. The Twelve Cards were printed letterpress from photopolymer plates on 100% cotton stock. The Weeks Chart was printed offset and folded by hand.

The edition is limited to five hundred numbered copies and twelve hors commerce. It will not be reprinted in this form.
