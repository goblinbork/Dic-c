The Twelve Axioms

  1. IThe Eulogy Begin at the end.
  2. IIThe Weeks A life is four thousand weeks. Spend them on purpose.
  3. IIIThe Question The quality of a life follows the quality of its questions.
  4. IVInversion Avoid stupidity before you attempt brilliance.
  5. VAsymmetry Make many small bets whose downside is bounded and whose upside is not.
  6. VIThe Loop You become whatever you get fast feedback on.
  7. VIIAttention Your experience is what you agree to attend to.
  8. VIIICompounding Small, repeated, for years, beats large, occasional, for months.
  9. IXTaste Quantity is the road to quality.
  10. XThe Ask Most of the world is a door that opens if you knock.
  11. XIThe Body The mind is something the body does.
  12. XIIThe Others Good relationships keep us happier and healthier. Period.
