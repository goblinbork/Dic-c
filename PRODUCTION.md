# THE INSTRUMENT — Production specification

Edition of 500 numbered copies plus 12 *hors commerce*, all signed. One printing. This document is the brief for the printer, binder, letterpress studio and box-maker, and the bill of materials behind the unit economics in `README.md`.

## 1. The book

| | Specification |
|---|---|
| Trim | 170 × 240 mm portrait |
| Extent | 96 pages, six 16-page signatures |
| Text paper | Munken Pure Rough 120 gsm (Arctic Paper; cream, uncoated, extra-rough surface; takes fountain-pen ink without feathering — test with the ledger pages). US alternative: Mohawk Superfine Softwhite Eggshell 100 lb text (148 gsm) |
| Printing | Offset, 2/2 throughout: black + one spot colour, "cinnabar", ≈ Pantone 1805 C (screen reference #B3261E). Tints of the spot at 30 % and 12 % are used for rules and grids. No process colour anywhere |
| Ink coverage | Light. Twelve openers carry a large solid-spot numeral; no solid pages |
| Binding | Smyth-sewn, square back, **hollow back** so the book opens flat for writing; rounded not required |
| Boards | 3 mm greyboard, square corners |
| Covering | Van Heek Textiles **Brillianta** book cloth, deep ink blue (choose from swatch; screen reference #1B2233). Alternative: Winter & Co Toile du Marais, linen look. Note: Bamberger Kaliko ceased production in 2022; do not specify it |
| Blocking | Front: title, subtitle and "One of five hundred" in copper metallic foil (Kurz Luxor range or equivalent); the dial mark **blind-debossed** (no foil), with its needle in copper foil. Spine: title top-to-bottom in copper; small mark at foot. Back: small mark in copper. Artwork: `book/dist/cover-case-wrap-PRINT.pdf`, sheet 2 (black = foil, magenta = blind deboss) |
| Spine width | Nominal 14 mm. **Confirm with a bulk sample** on the chosen stock before making dies |
| Endpapers | GF Smith Colorplan **Vermillion** 135 gsm, printed 1/0 black with the weeks grid (use `chart.html` at reduced scale), or plain if the printed option adds a second pass |
| Head and tail bands | Red and cream striped |
| Ribbons | Two, 6 mm: cinnabar and natural |
| Edges | Untrimmed look not required; plain trimmed edges. (Sprayed copper edges are an option at roughly +$1.50/copy) |
| Numbering | By hand, in ink, on the copyright page (page 4), by the publisher, before boxing. Copies 1–500 for sale; HC I–XII marked "H.C." |
| Files | `book/dist/the-instrument-PRINT.pdf` (bleed 3 mm, crop marks). Colours are supplied as RGB approximations from the build; **prepress must map text black to K 100 and #B3261E and its tints to the spot plate**. Fonts are embedded |

## 9. Before printing: checklist

1. Bulk sample on Munken Pure Rough 120 gsm → fix spine width → regenerate the case wrap (`cover.html`, `.spine` width and panel offsets).
2. Wet proof of one signature including an opener, a practice/ledger spread and a table page; check the 30 % tint holds on rough stock and that the ledger rules (0.3 mm) print.
3. Fountain-pen test on the proof stock.
4. Foil test: copper on the chosen cloth; check the 7 pt subtitle survives blocking (raise to 8 pt if it fills in).
5. Permissions: the epigraphs (Jobs, Seneca/Costa translation, Rilke/Norton translation, Jacobi attribution, Bezos, Feynman, James, Ravikant, Poincaré, Matthew 7:7, Van Dongen et al., Waldinger) and the Mary Oliver lines in the letter. All are short and attributed; confirm with counsel what needs a licence in the territories of sale.
6. Replace the placeholder imprint "Dic-c Editions" (title page, copyright page, cards, letter) with the final imprint, and add the ISBN and printer's credit to the colophon.
7. Register: a form keyed to copy number, a 512-row table, twelve scheduled one-word emails, and a transfer flow for the next owner.
8. Final read for typos with the print PDF; then export a final PDF/X-4 from the build for the printer, or have prepress convert.
