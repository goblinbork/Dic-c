# Changelog

## 1.1 — the plates edition (September 2026)

- Twelve technical plates, one per opener: the two regrets, a habit is a column, the fifty, the premortem, the survivable fraction (Kelly), loop length, residue, the flat part, the gap, the bridge, the deficit you cannot feel, the layers. Inline SVG, two inks, captioned with sources.
- Twelve thought experiments closing each essay.
- New pages: *A test, first* (a self-demonstrating opening on the contents verso), *The Year* (the whole method on one page), *After the year* (conclusion and second-year plan), *The Vocabulary* (thirty-one named tools with credits), *Acknowledgments* with a disclosure line.
- Named tools introduced in the text: the four failures, three bankrolls, the loop ratio, the room rule, the compounding audit, the small turn; Burt (2004) on brokers' ideas added to The Ask; Williamson & Feyer (2000) and Gilovich & Medvec (1994, 1995) added to the notes.
- `theme.css`: fonts, colours and sizes as variables read by every file; `tools/fetch-fonts.mjs` and `npm run fonts` to pull in Google Fonts; the build now prints a page map and warns when a chapter drifts off its six-page rhythm or the total is not a multiple of sixteen.
- `CLAUDE.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `EDITING.md` added. Ruled notes pages removed to keep the book at 96 pages.
